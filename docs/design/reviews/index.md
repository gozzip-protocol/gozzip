# Adversarial Reviews

Five independent, adversarial reviews of the protocol, each from a different discipline. They were written to stress the design, not to defend it, and they directly informed the 2026-07 simplification — see [ADR 019](../../decisions/019-documentation-simplification.md) for which recommendations were adopted, deferred, or declined.

- [Complexity](review-complexity.md) — is the protocol over-engineered for its stage? What to cut, simplify, or defer.
- [Protocol Design](review-protocol-design.md) — gossip mechanics, relay dependency, volume matching, and the WoT boundary.
- [Game Theory](review-game-theory.md) — Nash equilibria, cooperative stability, and incentive compatibility of the pact economy.
- [Cryptography](review-cryptography.md) — key hierarchy, forward-secrecy claims, and the proof-of-storage mechanism.
- [Deployment Reality](review-deployment-reality.md) — mobile background execution, app-store constraints, and production feasibility.

These are kept as a historical record; where a review's recommendation conflicts with the current design, the [decision records](../../decisions/index.md) are authoritative.
